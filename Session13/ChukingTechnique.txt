

=====================================CHUNKING===============================================

| Technique            | Main idea                              | Good use case            |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Fixed-size           | Split every N characters/tokens        | Simple baseline          |
| Fixed-size + overlap | Keep some content from previous chunk  | General RAG              |
| Sentence-based       | Split at sentence boundaries           | FAQs, policies           |
| Paragraph-based      | Split by paragraphs                    | Business documents       |
| Recursive            | Try paragraph → sentence → word        | General documents        |
| Semantic             | Split when meaning/topic changes       | Long knowledge documents |
| Structure-aware      | Split using headings/sections          | Markdown, policies       |
| Parent-child         | Small retrieval chunk + larger context | Enterprise RAG           |


# Global Corporate Policies and Guidelines

## Section 1: Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
Employees in eligible engineering, product, and corporate roles are permitted to work remotely up to three (3) days per week.
All remote employees must adhere to core working hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone to ensure synchronous collaboration.
The company provides a monthly internet and home office stipend of $50 USD per month for full-time remote employees.
Remote work from international locations requires prior approval from HR and Compliance at least 30 days in advance due to tax implications.

## Section 2: Annual Leave & Sick Time Policy
Full-time permanent employees receive twenty (15) days of paid annual leave (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly.
In addition, employees are entitled to ten (10) paid sick leave days per year for personal illness or family medical care.
A maximum of five (5) unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Unused carry-over days expire on March 31st if not taken.
Leave requests exceeding three (3) consecutive business days must be submitted at least two weeks in advance via the HR Portal.

## Section 3: Equipment & Hardware Policy
Every engineering employee is issued a primary workstation: either a 16-inch MacBook Pro (M-Series) or a Dell XPS 15 workstation.
Hardware replacement and refresh occurs automatically every three (3) years or upon hardware failure verified by IT Helpdesk.
Peripherals such as dual 27-inch 4K monitors, external keyboards, and ergonomic mice are provided upon request.
All lost, stolen, or damaged devices must be reported to IT Security within two (2) hours of discovery to initiate remote wipe protocols.

## Section 4: Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy
All domestic and international business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days prior to departure.
Air travel for domestic flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy / Main Cabin class. Business class is permitted for continuous flights exceeding 8 hours.
The maximum daily per diem meal allowance is $75 USD per day ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual expense item exceeding $25 USD. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of travel completion.

SMALL C# DEMO

The actual embedding generation can be done using an embedding model.

var sentences = new[]
{
    "Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave.",
    "Employees should submit leave requests 3 days in advance.",
    "Managers approve or reject leave requests.",
    "Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave.",
    "A medical certificate may be required for extended absence."
};

var embeddings = await embeddingService
    .GenerateEmbeddingsAsync(sentences);

Now compare neighboring embeddings:

for (int i = 0; i < embeddings.Count - 1; i++)
{
    double similarity =
        CosineSimilarity(
            embeddings[i],
            embeddings[i + 1]);

    Console.WriteLine(
        $"Sentence {i + 1} -> Sentence {i + 2} = {similarity:F2}");
}

Conceptually, you may get something like:

S1 -> S2 = 0.89
S2 -> S3 = 0.84
S3 -> S4 = 0.42   <-- Topic change
S4 -> S5 = 0.87

Then:

if (similarity < threshold)
{
    // Start a new chunk
}
else
{
    // Keep sentence in current chunk
}

For example:

double threshold = 0.70;

if (similarity < threshold)
{
    // New semantic chunk
}
