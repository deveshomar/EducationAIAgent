HR Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 annual leaves every calendar year.

Casual leave balance is 12.

Sick leave balance is 12.

Unused casual leaves cannot be carried forward.

----------------------------------------

Salary Policy

Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.

Salary slips are available on the employee portal.

Tax deductions follow government regulations.

----------------------------------------

IT Help Desk

For password reset contact extension 1001.

Laptop issues should be reported through ServiceNow.

VPN access requests require manager approval.

Email issues can be reported to ithelp@company.com.

----------------------------------------

Travel Policy

Employees can claim hotel expenses.

Manager approval is mandatory before booking flights.

Travel reimbursement should be submitted within 15 days.
