1.  Precision - out Of the chunks or document  
     I retrieved, how many were actually relevant?

        Precision = Relevant Retrieved Documents
        --------------------------------
             Total Retrieved Documents


       Retrieved = 5
       Relevant = 2

Precision = 2 / 5
          = 0.40
          = 40%

==> our retriever returned 5 chunks, but only 2 were useful.
==> Low precision = too much irrelevant information is being retrieved.

========================================================================


2.  Recall  (how much retrive out of total 
             valid document from DB)

Imagine there are 10 relevant documents in your database. (vector db)
Means , there are 10 documents that are relevant to the question in the database.

Your RAG retrieves 5 documents.

CASE 1
5 retrieved
5 relevant

Precision:  5/5 =100%

Recall  5/10  = 50%

========================================================

3.  . F1 Score - Balance of retrieval precision + recall
      Sometimes you want a single metric combining precision and recall.

Precision = Of the chunks retrieved, how many were relevant?
Recall = Of all the relevant chunks available, how many did we retrieve?
F1 = A single score balancing both.


F1 = 2 × Precision × Recall
     ------------------------
       Precision + Recall

Precision = 0.80
Recall    = 0.60

F1 = 2 × 0.80 × 0.60
     ------------------
        0.80 + 0.60

   = 0.686

4. Hit Rate@3-

Did the Top 3 contain at least one relevant chunk?

Means when we get Result after cosine simily search,
we check the top 3 results. If at least one of those 
results is relevant to the query, we consider it a hit. 
This metric helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our retrieval
system in providing useful information to users.
