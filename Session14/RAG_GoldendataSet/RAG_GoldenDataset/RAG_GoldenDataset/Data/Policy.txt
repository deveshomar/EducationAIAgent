GLOBAL CORPORATE EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK

==================================================
SECTION HR-01: GENERAL HR POLICY
==================================================

All full-time permanent employees must complete mandatory employee
onboarding within their first ten business days.

Employees must keep their personal information, emergency contact
details, bank information, and residential address updated in the
HR Portal.

Employees are expected to maintain professional conduct, protect
confidential company information, and follow all applicable company
policies.

Employees who change their legal name or other important personal
information must update the HR Portal within thirty days.

Managers are responsible for conducting regular one-on-one meetings
with their direct reports and completing required performance reviews.

==================================================
SECTION HR-02: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
==================================================

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to work eight hours per business day unless
their employment agreement specifies a different schedule.

Employees must record attendance using the company's approved
attendance system.

If an employee is unable to attend work because of an unexpected
emergency, the employee should notify their manager as soon as
reasonably possible.

Repeated unexplained attendance issues may be escalated to HR.

==================================================
SECTION LEAVE-01: ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

Full-time permanent employees receive twenty days of paid annual
leave per calendar year.

Annual leave is accrued monthly.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of five unused annual leave
days into the following calendar year.

Unused carried-forward leave expires on March 31.

Leave requests exceeding three consecutive business days must be
submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR Portal.

Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

==================================================
SECTION LEAVE-02: SICK LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

Employees receive ten paid sick leave days per calendar year.

Sick leave may be used for the employee's personal illness or
family medical care.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible when
they are unable to work because of illness.

Medical documentation may be requested by HR when required under
applicable company procedures.

Sick leave cannot be exchanged for cash.

==================================================
SECTION LEAVE-03: SPECIAL LEAVE
==================================================

Employees may request special leave for approved personal or
family circumstances.

Special leave requires manager approval and may require HR approval
depending on the type and duration of leave.

Employees should submit special leave requests through the HR Portal.

==================================================
SECTION IT-01: IT HELP DESK POLICY
==================================================

Employees should contact the IT Help Desk for company laptop,
desktop, software, network, VPN, email, and approved application
issues.

Employees should provide a clear description of the issue,
relevant screenshots when available, and the asset number when
reporting a hardware problem.

The IT Help Desk assigns a priority to each incident based on
business impact and urgency.

Critical incidents affecting a large number of employees may be
escalated to the IT infrastructure team.

Employees must not install unauthorized software on company
devices.

==================================================
SECTION IT-02: PASSWORD AND ACCOUNT ISSUES
==================================================

Employees who forget their corporate password should use the
approved password-reset process or contact the IT Help Desk.

Employees must never share their corporate password with another
person.

If an employee suspects that their account has been compromised,
they must immediately contact the IT Help Desk and Information
Security.

Multi-factor authentication must remain enabled for corporate
accounts where required.

==================================================
SECTION IT-03: HARDWARE AND DEVICE ISSUES
==================================================

Engineering employees are issued a primary company workstation.

Supported workstation options include a 16-inch M-Series MacBook Pro
and a Dell XPS 15 workstation.

Hardware replacement or refresh normally occurs every three years
or when a hardware failure is verified by the IT Help Desk.

Dual 27-inch 4K monitors, external keyboards, and ergonomic mice
may be provided upon request.

Lost, stolen, or damaged company devices must be reported to
IT Security within two hours of discovery.

==================================================
SECTION IT-04: REMOTE WORK TECHNICAL SUPPORT
==================================================

Employees working remotely should contact the IT Help Desk when
experiencing VPN connectivity, corporate Wi-Fi, authentication,
remote desktop, or approved collaboration-tool issues.

Employees should provide the time the problem started and the
error message when submitting a remote-work technical incident.

==================================================
SECTION SALARY-01: SALARY POLICY
==================================================

Employee salaries are paid monthly.

Salary payments are normally deposited into the employee's
registered bank account.

Employees must keep their bank account information updated in the
HR Portal.

Salary slips are made available through the employee self-service
portal.

Employees should contact HR Payroll if they identify an incorrect
salary payment or missing salary slip.

==================================================
SECTION SALARY-02: SALARY REVISION
==================================================

Salary revisions are based on company compensation processes,
employee performance, role expectations, internal equity, and
business considerations.

A salary revision is not automatically guaranteed every year.

Employees should discuss compensation questions with their manager
or HR.

==================================================
SECTION SEPARATION-01: RESIGNATION POLICY
==================================================

Employees who wish to resign must submit a formal resignation
through the company's approved resignation process.

The resignation date and proposed last working day must be clearly
specified.

Employees should continue performing their normal responsibilities
during the notice period unless otherwise instructed.

==================================================
SECTION SEPARATION-02: NOTICE PERIOD
==================================================

The applicable notice period is determined by the employee's
employment agreement and applicable company policy.

Employees should consult their employment agreement or HR when they
are unsure about their applicable notice period.

Early release from the notice period requires appropriate management
and HR approval.

==================================================
SECTION SEPARATION-03: EXIT PROCESS
==================================================

Before the last working day, employees must return company property
including laptops, access cards, security tokens, and other assigned
equipment.

Employees must complete knowledge transfer activities requested by
their manager.

IT will disable or remove access to company systems according to
the separation process.

HR will complete the required exit documentation and coordinate the
exit process.

==================================================
SECTION SEPARATION-04: FINAL SETTLEMENT
==================================================

Final settlement includes applicable salary, approved reimbursements,
and other amounts payable under company policy and the employee's
employment agreement.

The timing of final settlement depends on completion of required
exit activities and applicable payroll processing.

Employees should contact HR Payroll for questions regarding their
final settlement.

==================================================
SECTION TRAVEL-01: BUSINESS TRAVEL
==================================================

All domestic and international business travel must be booked
through the corporate travel portal at least fourteen days before
departure.

Domestic flights under six hours must be booked in Economy or
Main Cabin class.

Business class is permitted for continuous flights exceeding
eight hours.

==================================================
SECTION EXPENSE-01: TRAVEL EXPENSES
==================================================

The maximum daily meal per diem is seventy-five US dollars.

The meal allowance is fifteen dollars for breakfast, twenty-five
dollars for lunch, and thirty-five dollars for dinner.

Itemized receipts are mandatory for individual expense items
exceeding twenty-five US dollars.

Expense claims must be submitted within thirty days of travel
completion.

==================================================
SECTION SECURITY-01: INFORMATION SECURITY
==================================================

Employees must protect confidential company information and must
not share company credentials.

Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents to
Information Security.

Company devices must use approved security controls and software.

Employees must not disable required security protections.
